## [0.1.0] - 2026-07-27

### Added

- Concurrency-safe fixed-window limiter with sharded state.
- Bounded key cardinality and hashed key storage.
- Explicit decisions, cleanup, reset, capacity handling, and lifecycle management.
- Dependency-free standalone HTTP service.
- Unit, race, fuzz, integration, and benchmark coverage.
